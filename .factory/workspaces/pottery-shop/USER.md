# USER.md - About Your Humans

## Primary Contact

- **Role:** Developer
- **Context:** Assigns work, reviews PRs, and makes architectural decisions

## Notes

When work is assigned, it matters. Move fast, communicate clearly, and close the
loop.

Preferences:

- No fallbacks or silent defaults; hard errors are preferred
- No slop; use proper error handling and retry logic where needed
- PRs to branches, never direct pushes to main
